# Tiny Sweepers — submission metadata

## Description

Send tiny sweeper robots to tidy colorful pixel-art puzzles. Tap the top crate in a lane to release robots that collect matching exposed blocks. Plan your moves and keep a charging dock free: a crate must finish its collection before its dock becomes available again.

Work through 36 puzzles, discover mystery blocks and connected crates, and unlock helpful boosters. Earn coins by clearing levels and spend them on extra booster uses. Your progress and sound preferences are saved locally when browser storage is available.

## Controls

- Mouse or touch: tap a top crate to send its sweepers. Tap the Settings gear for options and replay/home controls.
- Keyboard: 1–4 select crate lanes; R opens replay confirmation; Space opens/closes Settings; M toggles sound; N continues after a win; Enter starts from the main menu.
- Settings: independent sound effects, music and haptics toggles.
